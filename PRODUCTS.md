## Product Information Structure

Each product will have:
- **SKU**: Unique product identifier
- **Product Name**: Full product description
- **Category**: Product category
- **Brand**: Manufacturer/supplier brand
- **Price**: Retail price in ZMW (Zambian Kwacha)
- **Stock Quantity**: Current inventory level
- **Reorder Level**: Minimum stock before reorder
- **Supplier**: Primary supplier
- **Vehicle Compatibility**: List of compatible vehicle models
- **Part Number**: OEM or equivalent part number
- **Description**: Detailed description
- **Image**: Product photo
- **Warranty**: Warranty period/terms
- **Date Added**: When added to inventory

## Pricing Strategy

- **Cost Price**: Wholesale cost from supplier
- **Retail Price**: Marked-up selling price
- **Wholesale Price**: Discounted rate for bulk orders
- **Seasonal Discounts**: Applied during promotions
- **Loyalty Discounts**: For regular customers

## Stock Management

- **Fast-Moving Items**: Weekly orders (engine oil, filters, spark plugs)
- **Standard Items**: Bi-weekly orders
- **Slow-Moving Items**: Monthly or as-needed orders
- **Discontinued Items**: Clear before removing from catalog
